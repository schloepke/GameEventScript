The standard geometry library: the external types :Box, :Poly and :Disc over GesBox, GesPoly and GesDisc, and the extension functions :geometry.*, so that hosts and scripts compute alike. A host that wants it passes Catalog(IGesExternalTypeCatalog?) to the compiler and ExternalTypes(IGesExternalTypeRegistry?) and Extensions(IGesExtensionRegistry?) to the host, each in front of its own catalog or registry, which resolves what the library does not.

Remarks

:Box(lower: p, upper: q) takes two corners in any order, which its fields lower and upper return ordered (min and max are GES keywords), and :Box(center: p, size: v) a centre and a size; :Poly(points: [...]) takes its points; :Disc(center: p, radius: r) a centre and a radius and :Disc(radius: r) a radius around the origin; the radius follows its declared Number type, so a Percentage is its unitless ratio. Corners, points, sizes and radii share one unit, which the value keeps; another kind or a mixed unit constructs nothing. A polygon or disc need not be valid to exist, but its operations give nothing when it is not. Functions: size, center, union, inset(, by:) and nearest, the point of a shape nearest to a point (clamp is a GES keyword), of boxes; bounds, area (signed and unitless, like dot), distance and centroid of polygons; center, bounds, area, distance, inset(, by:) and nearest of discs; contains and intersects of all, a box with a polygon counting as its outline in the plane and a disc meeting a 2D box. Anything else, such as another unit, gives nothing.
